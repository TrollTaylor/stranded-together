# Fragile's edited textures between two games on the launcher's own Local test files (docs/textures.md; docs/real-partner-
# body.md section 29; design T2.2), hidden. The run's own textures folder (the extractor reads the game read-only into
# <run>\textures; both games' session.ini name it): bold generated patterns (no game data) in "16 Gold" (checker) and
# "09 Mustard" (stripes); every other colour folder untouched. The guest (Fragile) builds her look from the folder of her
# Sam's suit colour and wears it in her self view; the host gets the look over the link (STT1, channel 5), validates every
# blob and shows it on his partner Fragile. host_save: the guest's Sam starts as a copy of the host's (gold, 16). Then the
# guest's colour goes to 9 (stripes, sent too), 0 (her own suit on both screens) and 16 again (the host has it: no new
# transfer). The host's own Sam stays gold.
#   pwsh -File tools\testenv\Start-TestRun.ps1 -Scenario two-games-fragile-textures -SeedSaves complete-100 -GuestSeed profile-only
role host
graphics low
timeout 360
launcherini local-test
textures extract
textures pattern checker 16 Gold
textures pattern stripes 09 Mustard
start control 200
@guest waitlog 60 \[self\] showing 'fragile_suit' in place of Sam .*fit on
waitlog 60 \[sync\] partner body: real 'fragile_suit'
@both goto mknot-road
wait 6000
@guest pad none 2400 ly=32767
@guest pad none 900 ly=-32767
@guest waitlog 60 \[tex\] body self .*: look .* bound .* kept [1-9]
@guest waitlog 30 \[tex\] look #[0-9]+ sent: colour 16, 1 blob
waitlog 150 \[tex\] partner look .*: received S0 .* valid
waitlog 30 \[tex\] body partner .*: look .* bound .* kept [1-9]
wait 1500
@both action tex 0
screenshot 00-host-sees-guest-gold
@guest screenshot 00-guest-self-gold
# mustard: the stripes, sent too (the host's checks first: they come after the guest's own)
@guest action real_outfit 4 9
waitlog 150 \[tex\] partner look .*: complete for colour 9
waitnew 30 \[tex\] body partner .*: look .* bound .* kept [1-9]
@both action tex 0
@guest waitnew 10 \[tex\] bodies: self [0-9A-F]+ look [0-9a-f]{8} overrides 2
wait 1500
# the host stands up (an idle Sam sits down during the transfer) and looks ahead again
pad none 500 ly=32767
wait 3000
pad none 300 rx=32767
wait 1000
screenshot 01-host-sees-guest-mustard
@guest screenshot 01-guest-self-mustard
# 0: nothing edited in "00 Default": her own suit on both screens
@guest action real_outfit 4 0
waitnew 30 \[tex\] partner look: her own suit \(colour 0\)
waitnew 30 \[tex\] body partner .*: look .* taken off
wait 1000
@both action tex 0
@guest waitnew 10 \[tex\] bodies: self [0-9A-F]+ look - overrides 0
wait 500
# the host stands up (an idle Sam sits down during the transfer) and looks ahead again
pad none 500 ly=32767
wait 3000
pad none 300 rx=32767
wait 1000
screenshot 02-host-sees-guest-own
@guest screenshot 02-guest-self-own
# gold again: the host has the texture already (no transfer)
@guest action real_outfit 4 16
waitnew 60 \[tex\] partner look .*: complete for colour 16 .*cache hit 1
waitnew 30 \[tex\] body partner .*: look .* bound .* kept [1-9]
wait 1000
@both action tex 0
@guest waitnew 10 \[tex\] bodies: self [0-9A-F]+ look [0-9a-f]{8} overrides 2
wait 500
# the host stands up (an idle Sam sits down during the transfer) and looks ahead again
pad none 500 ly=32767
wait 3000
pad none 300 rx=32767
wait 1000
screenshot 03-host-sees-guest-gold-again
@guest screenshot 03-guest-self-gold-again
# the host's own Sam: still gold (his outfit line), never touched by the textures
action real_outfit 0
pad none 1300 rx=32767
wait 1500
screenshot 04-host-own-sam
# tex 7 on the host (review fix): the partner look's RAM copies are let go of and the same look shown again (read from the
# disk cache when needed), never her own suit for good
action tex 7
waitnew 10 \[tex\] host: tex 7: [0-9]+ of the guest's 1 blob\(s\) let go of from RAM .*look #[0-9]+ shown again
wait 1500
@both action tex 0
waitnew 10 \[tex\] bodies: .*partner [0-9A-F]+ look [0-9a-f]{8} overrides 2
@both action tex 0
nolog \[tex\] .*FAULTED
@guest nolog \[tex\] .*FAULTED
nolog \[tex\] refused
@guest nolog \[tex\] host refused
nolog \[tex\] .*NOT valid
nolog \[tex\] body self
@both action log_status
@guest quit
wait 3000
quit
