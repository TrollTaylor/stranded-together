# Vehicle sync between two games for all three vehicle kinds (docs/real-partner-body.md section 22), both directions,
# with the overlap and the robustness cases. The host plays Sam, the guest Fragile in her porter suit with the self view
# (as the launcher writes it). Both games load the same 100% save, so each world has its own copy of every parked
# vehicle: the watcher's own vehicle stands where the driver's copy first appears (the overlap case: the copy hidden
# until the driver has moved it away, vehicle_overlap_hide).
# 0. The garage (Episode 15, Mountain Knot City): the host gets on its purple roadster and drives off, the guest watches
#    from beyond its own roadster (the roadster's copy, its hologram: no solid roadster exists, section 22.11; hidden over
#    it at first, also put 20 m below). Since section 22.11 the trike (also the Kusanagi) and the truck are the SOLID copies
#    by default, their collision turned off on each copy (ehicle_sync 21 logs it on the watcher after each drive).
# 1. Outside (the game's vehicle registry: `vehicle_sync 8 <side m> <4 Kusanagi | 2 truck | 3 trike>` places Sam beside
#    the nearest of that kind facing it, X gets him on; the watcher stands 9-10 m beside its own copy of that vehicle and
#    turns its camera onto it, `vehicle_sync 18 <kind>` + right-stick pulses until `camera aimed`): the host drives the Kusanagi
#    trike; its sends are held 3 s mid-drive (`vehicle_sync 10 3000`: the copy is held where it stands, then plays on),
#    then 12 s while it drives on (the partner body goes after 10 s of silence, the copy with it; both come back where
#    the host is then); it stops and gets off.
# 2. The guest drives the truck, the host watches.
# 3. The host backs the standard trike out of the rocks it is parked against (LT) and drives it, the guest watches.
#   pwsh -File tools\testenv\Start-TestRun.ps1 -Scenario two-games-vehicle-trike -SeedSaves complete-100
