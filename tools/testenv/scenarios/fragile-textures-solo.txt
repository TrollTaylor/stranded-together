# Fragile's edited textures from the user's folder (docs/textures.md; docs/real-partner-body.md section 29; design T2.1),
# solo, hidden. The run gets its own textures folder: the launcher's extractor reads the game's archives (read-only) into
# <run>\textures, then st_textool paints a bold generated checker (no game data) over "16 Gold"'s editable pictures, as a
# user would. The self view's fragile_suit body wears the colour folder of our Sam's suit colour (the 100% save: gold):
# the checker on her suit only (boots, neck, badge stay hers). Silver (15, no edit): her own suit (the v0.23 tint is off by
# default). Gold again; then the folder's picture is replaced by stripes while the game runs (hot reload); the feature off
# and on (tex 4). A texture nobody wears is released after 5 s (tex 6: at once) and made again when needed.
#   pwsh -File tools\testenv\Start-TestRun.ps1 -Scenario fragile-textures-solo -SeedSaves complete-100
role solo
timeout 330
# round 8: this scenario proves the v0.24 rule (an unpainted folder is her own suit): ready-made colours off
ini textures_defaults=0
ini partner_body=real
ini pose_hook=1
ini my_character=fragile_suit
ini self_character=1
ini self_feet_hide=0
textures extract
textures pattern checker 16 Gold
start
waitlog 40 \[self\] showing 'fragile_suit'
waitlog 30 \[tex\] ready: manifest table
waitlog 60 \[tex\] look .* for colour 16 .*: 1 slot\(s\) edited \(S0\)
waitlog 30 \[tex\] body self .*: look .* bound .* kept [1-9]
pad none 1300 rx=32767
wait 1500
action tex 0
screenshot 01-gold-checker
# silver: nothing edited in "15 Silver": her own suit
action real_outfit 5 15
waitnew 30 \[tex\] body self .*: look .* taken off
wait 1500
screenshot 02-silver-own
# gold again (the texture is kept: no new clone)
action real_outfit 5 16
waitnew 30 \[tex\] body self .*: look .* bound .* kept [1-9]
wait 1500
screenshot 03-gold-again
# the user saves another picture while the game runs
textures pattern stripes 16 Gold
waitnew 30 \[tex\] reload: colour 16
# no flicker (review fix): the old look stays on until the new one is made, then one swap (never her own look between)
waitnew 30 \[tex\] body self .*: look .* taken off \([0-9]+ override\(s\) undone, look change\)
waitnew 30 \[tex\] body self .*: look .* bound .* kept [1-9]
wait 1500
screenshot 04-gold-stripes
# the checker texture nobody wears any more is released after 5 s (textures_free, the game's own Texture release)
waitnew 15 \[tex\] clone S0 .* released \(unused for 5 s
# off and on
action tex 4 0
waitnew 10 \[tex\] body self .*: look .* taken off
wait 1500
screenshot 05-off
# released at once while off (tex 6); on again: made again from the blob in RAM
action tex 6
waitnew 10 \[tex\] clone S0 .* released \(tex 6
action tex 4 1
waitnew 15 \[tex\] body self .*: look .* bound
wait 1500
screenshot 06-on-again
action tex 0
nolog \[tex\] .*FAULTED
nolog \[tex\] refused
nolog \[tex\] .*NOT USED
quit
