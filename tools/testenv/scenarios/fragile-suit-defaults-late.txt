# The launcher's extraction running while the game runs (round 9 review; docs/textures.md "Get the pictures"), solo,
# hidden. The game starts on an EMPTY textures folder (her own suit). The run's folder is then extracted in the
# background (`textures extract-start`: the launcher's own extractor, the same child its automatic extraction starts)
# and the guest's Sam changes to 09 Mustard at once: while the extraction holds the folder's .extract.lock the mod waits
# (no look built from a half-written folder, the current one stays); when it ends, 09 is built from the new index:
# the ready-made mustard (S0 only: 01..14 differ from her own suit in the Colour only), no restart. Noon, clear, on
# the knot road; a debug Fragile next to Sam.
#   pwsh -File tools\testenv\Run-Batch.ps1 -Scenarios fragile-suit-defaults-late -SeedSaves complete-100
role solo
timeout 300
ini partner_body=real
ini pose_hook=1
ini my_character=fragile_suit
ini self_character=0
start
waitplayer 60
goto mknot-road
?action set_time_of_day 12
?action dev_weather 0
wait 3000
pad none 2400 ly=32767
pad none 900 ly=-32767
wait 1000
action real_outfit 4 16
action real_spawn 16 -1.1 0
waitlog 60 \[real\] 'fragile_suit' is live
# not extracted yet: her own suit (no manifest)
waitlog 30 \[tex\] no \.manifest\.txt in the textures folder
?action set_time_of_day 12
wait 2000
screenshot late-16-own-before
# the extraction in the background; a colour change meanwhile waits for its end
textures extract-start
# (the extraction opens the game's archives first, then holds the folder's lock)
wait 1500
action real_outfit 4 9
waitnew 15 \[tex\] the launcher is extracting the textures: her looks wait for it to end
wait 1000
screenshot late-09-during
nolog \[tex\] look .* for colour 9
textures extract-wait
waitnew 30 \[tex\] the launcher's extraction ended after
waitnew 30 \[tex\] look .* for colour 9 .*ready-made \(S0\)
waitnew 20 \[tex\] body debug .*: look .* bound
?action set_time_of_day 12
wait 2000
screenshot late-09-after
# and back to 16: the ready-made gold from the new index
action real_outfit 4 16
waitnew 30 \[tex\] look .* for colour 16 .*ready-made \(S0 S1\)
waitnew 20 \[tex\] body debug .*: look .* bound
wait 1500
screenshot late-16-after
nolog \[tex\] .*FAULTED
nolog \[tex\] refused
quit
