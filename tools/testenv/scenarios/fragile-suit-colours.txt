# Fragile's porter suit in Sam's suit colours (docs/real-partner-body.md section 27), solo, hidden: the self view's
# fragile_suit body (the launcher's guest default) from the front while the colour changes. The 100% save's Sam wears
# gold (16), so she starts gold by herself; then the debug override (`real_outfit 5 <c>`, purely visual on our own body)
# walks silver, palette 9 (olive), her own look (0), palette 1, 4, 13, gold again; then our own Sam's colour byte
# (`real_outfit 4 <c>`, hidden tests only) shows that she follows it live.
#   pwsh -File tools\testenv\Start-TestRun.ps1 -Scenario fragile-suit-colours -SeedSaves complete-100
role solo
timeout 300

wait 1200
screenshot 04-own
