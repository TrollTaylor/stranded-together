# Shots P3-0 (docs/enemy-sync.md P3a, plan R1a): does a CreateBullet replay draw the game's own muzzle flash, trail and impacts,
# and can its damage be told apart (the replay set, or the bullet system's dummy owner)? Solo on the 100% save, its assault
# rifle (the wheel's upper left), on the road by Mountain Knot City. Our own shots are echoed 2.5 m to our right through the
# whole replay path (shots_echo), so one game shows the original and the replay side by side; then one replay at our own Sam
# without the filter (dev_god 0: its damage proves the identity) and one with it; then 200 replays (the attack-context leak
# check: the working set before and after).
#   pwsh -File tools\testenv\Run-Batch.ps1 -Scenarios shots-probe -SeedSaves complete-100
# A probe (research: the echo, the test replays and the leak check); the shots proof is two-games-shots.
# covers: shots
# select: probe
role solo
graphics low
timeout 330
ini shots_sync=1
start
goto mknot-road
?action dev_god 2
waitlog 10 \[shots\] byte checks: [0-9]+/[0-9]+ ok
waitlog 5 \[shots\] ON: Enqueue hooked, Sam filter on, NPC drop on
waitlog 20 \[shots\] registry: [1-9][0-9]* weapons, [1-9][0-9]* magazines
# the rifle from the wheel: D-pad right held, the stick to the upper left, centred, let go
pad dpad_right 600 keep
pad dpad_right 900 lx=-23170 ly=23170 rx=-23170 ry=23170 keep
pad dpad_right 400 keep
pad none 0
wait 2500
action shots_echo 1 2.5
# aim, the camera a little down the road, a burst
pad none 1200 lt=255 keep
pad none 250 lt=255 ry=-14000 keep
pad none 500 lt=255 rt=255 keep
screenshot 01-burst-echo-a
pad none 120 lt=255 rt=255 keep
screenshot 01-burst-echo-b
pad none 120 lt=255 rt=255 keep
screenshot 01-burst-echo-c
pad none 120 lt=255 rt=255 keep
screenshot 01-burst-echo-d
pad none 200 lt=255 keep
screenshot 02-impacts
pad none 0
waitlog 8 \[shots\] sent n=[1-9]
waitlog 8 \[shots\] replayed n=[1-9][0-9]* \(muzzle [1-9]
action shots_echo 0
action shots 0
# the damage identity: a replay at our own Sam, the filter left out (its damage lands), then with it
?action dev_god 0
action shots_test 6 1
waitnew 6 \[shots\] test: ref match=1 .*kept \(nofilter\)
?action dev_refill
action shots_test 6 0
waitnew 6 \[shots\] test: ref match=1 .*dropped by the filter \(ref match\)
?action dev_refill
?action dev_god 2
# 200 replays into the road ahead: the working set
action shots_test 0 16 200
waitlog 40 \[shots\] leak test: [0-9]+ replays; working set
screenshot 03-after-leak-test
action shots 0
nolog \[shots\] FAULT
nolog \[shots\] byte check FAILED
nolog \[gate\] OTGC WRITE
