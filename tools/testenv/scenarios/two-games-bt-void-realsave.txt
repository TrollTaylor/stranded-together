# Round 31 stage 6 gate (the round 31 build spec, Stage 6; module bt_void): leg 3, the host_save=0 leg (the blast only from 300 m; no crater).
# Leg 3 (the host_save=0 rules, bt_void=2 on both): a launcherini scenario cannot run a real-save guest (the launcher's Local
# test writes host_save=1 and testenv refuses another host_save line), so the guest turns on bt_void's real-save stand-in
# (bt_sync 9 1 4, hidden runs only): its blast and crater gates then act as on a host_save=0 guest (only stricter: nothing else
# changes). The host's catcher-flagged test VOID2 at 600 m: the guest plays the blast (from 300 m) and refuses the crater
# ("this game plays on its own real save"); then 150 m: the blast is dropped ("within 300 m"). The stand-in goes off at the end.
#   pwsh -File tools\testenv\Run-Batch.ps1 -Scenarios two-games-bt-void-realsave -SeedSaves complete-100 -GuestSeed profile-only -Id round31-bt-void
# From the launcher-generated ini (launcherini local-test) plus the key under test. That file is host_save=1 (a sandboxed
# guest) and testenv refuses an added host_save line, so this leg uses bt_void's real-save stand-in (below).
# The guest's lines right after an action are waitlog (each is the run's only one): the guest logs a dropped blast in the tick
# it receives the VOID2, before the host's waitnew step starts (run 20261128-012219: the drop line was written during the action
# step, so the waitnew after the host's could not see it).
# covers: bt-void
role host
graphics low
timeout 360
launcherini local-test
ini bt_sync=2
ini bt_void=2
start control 200
waitlog 60 \[vshare\] host: world ready
@guest waitlog 60 \[vshare\] guest: world ready
waitlog 5 \[bt\] sync: bt_sync=2 as the host
@guest waitlog 5 \[bt\] sync: bt_sync=2 as the guest
waitlog 60 \[bt\] sync: partner hello: bt_sync 2, role guest
@guest waitlog 60 \[bt\] sync: partner hello: bt_sync 2, role host
waitlog 5 \[bt\] void: partner hello: VOID2 yes
@guest waitlog 5 \[bt\] void: partner hello: VOID2 yes
@both action dev_god 2
wait 3000
@guest action bt_sync 9 1 4
@guest waitnew 5 \[bt\] void: real-save stand-in ON on this game
action bt_sync 9 600 3
waitnew 5 \[bt\] void: test VOID2 #[0-9]+ at .*: (599|600|601) m from the partner's body.*a catcher crater r 40 wanted: sent
@guest waitlog 10 \[bt\] void: partner voidout blast at .* (59[5-9]|60[0-5]) m from you: far resource, flash fade 150/450 m
@guest waitlog 10 \[bt\] void: crater not added: this game plays on its own real save \(host_save=0\): no crater record
@guest waitnew 20 \[bt\] void: partner voidout blast done \(16 s\): ref released
wait 2000
action bt_sync 9 150 1
waitnew 5 \[bt\] void: test VOID2 #[0-9]+ at .*: (149|150|151) m from the partner's body.*: sent
@guest waitlog 10 \[bt\] void: partner voidout blast dropped: within 300 m of you or at an unknown distance \(real save: the near blast is untested\)
@guest action bt_sync 9 0 4
@guest waitnew 5 \[bt\] void: real-save stand-in off on this game
@guest nolog \[bt\] void: crater record added
nolog \[bt\] void: .*FAULTED
@guest nolog \[bt\] void: .*FAULTED
nolog \[bt\] void: byte check FAILED
@guest nolog \[bt\] void: byte check FAILED
nolog \[bt\] void: .*NOT sent
@guest nolog \[bt\] void: voidout flash window closed .*(changed|FAILED|did not finish)
nolog \[gate\] OTGC WRITE
@guest nolog \[gate\] OTGC WRITE
@guest quit
wait 3000
quit
