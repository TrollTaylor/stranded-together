# Shared real vehicles, persistence (design M2.10 #6; docs/real-partner-body.md section 23.13), hidden:
#   pwsh -File tools\testenv\Start-TestRun.ps1 -Scenario two-games-veh-persist -SeedSaves complete-100 -GuestSeed profile-only
# The guest drives a host truck about 100 m and parks it; it comes to rest and goes back to the host (RELEASE at rest,
# GRANT handback). The host's world followed it all the way, so the host's game's own persisted entry for it (the save
# writer 0x142F7CF20, vehicle_share 8) holds the parked spot: that is what the host's next save writes. The mod has no
# forced save (design section 6, item 11), so the reload half of M2.10 #6 (save, reload, the vehicle still there) needs
# a save the game makes by itself; the T2 dumps and the entries are the check here. Pass: the truck at the parked spot
# in both worlds (dumps T2, within 0.5 m), its persisted entry there.
# covers: vehicle-share host-save

graphics low
timeout 360
ini host_save=1
ini partner_body=real
ini partner_collision=0
waitready 90
@guest waitready 120
waitlog 120 \[overlay\] input attached
@guest waitlog 120 \[overlay\] input attached
waitlog 10 \[coop\] host-held save session ON \(host
@guest waitlog 10 \[coop\] host-held save session ON \(guest
waitlog 60 \[vshare\] shared real vehicles ON \(host
@guest waitlog 60 \[vshare\] shared real vehicles ON \(guest
waitlog 10 \[vdrive\] remote driving ON \(host
@guest waitlog 10 \[vdrive\] remote driving ON \(guest
# the title menus (~20 s after the first frame); the host Continues (CONTINUE is selected on its title)
wait 22000
waitlog 30 \[link\] CONNECTED
pad A 150
waitlog 30 \[coop\] host: title Continue
@guest waitlog 120 \[coop\] guest: wrote the host's save #1 as manualsave5
until 240 5000 \[sync\] local player entity 0+ -> 0*[1-9A-F] => pad A 120
waitplayer 60
@guest waitlog 180 \[coop\] guest: in the world after
@guest waitplayer 60
waitlog 60 \[vshare\] host: world ready
@guest waitlog 60 \[vshare\] guest: world ready
@guest waitlog 60 \[vshare\] guest: the host's snapshot #[0-9]+ is complete
@guest waitlog 30 \[vshare\] guest: binding:

@guest action vehicle_sync 8 1.8 2

wait 1500

@guest action vehicle_dump 1
@guest until 20 4000 \[vdrive\] guest: our Sam drives truck => pad X 150

waitlog 20 \[vdrive\] follower of truck 0x[0-9A-F]+ attached
@guest pad none 6000 rt=220 keep

@guest pad none 4000 rt=220 keep

?waitlog 30 \[vdrive\] host: the guest releases truck 0x[0-9A-F]+ \(at rest
?@guest waitlog 10 \[vdrive\] guest: GRANT for truck 0x[0-9A-F]+: owner host

@guest action vehicle_dump 2

@guest until 25 150 camera aimed #1 => pad none 200 rx=32767

# 20261119-120115)

@guest nolog \[vdrive\].*FAULT
