# Round 28 defaults with "Show my partner's gunfire" unticked and no enemy sync (round 28 defaults review: the SOD1 / BTS1 hellos
# wait for the partner's SEH1 beacon, which only the shots hooks sent). The launcher's own Local test files plus shots_sync=0
# (what the unticked box writes) and enemy_sync=0, so the shots hooks stay out on both games; the beacon now goes alone
# (shot_sync.cpp g_beacon_want, parts 0). Checks: the beacon-only line, the PHIT relay off (it needs enemy sync), the Odradek
# scan and BT sync hellos both ways, and one real host scan shown on the guest's screen with the shipped keys (odradek_probe=0).
#   pwsh -File tools\testenv\Run-Batch.ps1 -Scenarios two-games-round28-gunfire-off -SeedSaves complete-100 -GuestSeed profile-only -Id round28-gunfire-off
# covers: odradek-scan bt-sync
role host
graphics low
timeout 240
launcherini local-test
ini shots_sync=0
ini enemy_sync=0
start control 200
waitlog 60 \[vshare\] host: world ready
@guest waitlog 60 \[vshare\] guest: world ready
# ---- no shots hooks, the beacon alone; the PHIT relay off without enemy sync
waitlog 5 \[shots\] off \(shots_sync=0\); the SEH1 beacon still goes
@guest waitlog 5 \[shots\] off \(shots_sync=0\); the SEH1 beacon still goes
waitlog 5 \[phit\] relay off: enemy_partner_melee=1 needs enemy sync
@guest waitlog 5 \[phit\] relay off: enemy_partner_melee=1 needs enemy sync
waitlog 10 \[odra\] detour installed .*scan sync on
@guest waitlog 10 \[odra\] detour installed .*scan sync on
# ---- the hellos both ways (each after the other's beacon)
waitlog 60 \[odra\] hello sent caps=0x01
@guest waitlog 60 \[odra\] hello sent caps=0x01
waitlog 30 \[odra\] partner hello caps=0x01
@guest waitlog 30 \[odra\] partner hello caps=0x01
waitlog 60 \[bt\] sync: partner hello: bt_sync 2, role guest
@guest waitlog 60 \[bt\] sync: partner hello: bt_sync 2, role host
# ---- a real scan: the host's own, shown on the guest's screen (20 m apart)
@both action dev_god 2
goto mknot-road
@guest goto -1197.2 291.5 0
wait 4000
action odra 2
waitnew 5 \[odra\] scan triggered
waitlog 10 \[odra\] partner scan sent seq=[0-9]+ origin=
@guest waitlog 10 \[odra\] partner scan received seq=[0-9]+ origin=
@guest waitlog 10 \[odra\] partner scan shown origin=.* mode=full
wait 500
@guest screenshot 10-guest-sees-host-scan
wait 5000
action odra 7
waitnew 5 \[odra\] scan sync: key 1, start path on .*partner hello caps 0x01.*channel 6 known
@guest action odra 7
@guest waitnew 5 \[odra\] scan sync: key 1, start path on .*partner hello caps 0x01.*channel 6 known
# ---- the end
nolog \[shots\] FAULT
@guest nolog \[shots\] FAULT
nolog \[odra\] .*FAULT
@guest nolog \[odra\] .*FAULT
nolog \[odra\] partner scan not sent
@guest nolog \[odra\] partner scan not sent
nolog \[bt\] sync: no partner hello
@guest nolog \[bt\] sync: no partner hello
@both action dev_god 0
@guest quit
wait 3000
quit
