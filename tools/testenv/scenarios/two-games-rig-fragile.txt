# The suit rig on Fragile between two games (round 20 stage 3; docs/real-partner-body.md section 41), hidden, on the
# launcher's own Local test files (host Sam, guest Fragile with the self view):
#   pwsh -File tools\testenv\Run-Batch.ps1 -Scenarios two-games-rig-fragile -SeedSaves complete-100 -GuestSeed profile-only -Id round20-stage3
# The guest's Sam (a copy of the host's 100% save) wears the backpack, the BB pod, the speed skeleton and the hip crates in
# the porter suit; the host's copy of her (the partner slot's exo carrier) shows the suit's hardware that joins that gear to
# her: the pod's chest mount (46) and pipe (2), the backpack's straps and hip belt (25-28, the stand-in for the belt entity
# the partner body never gets) and the hip rig (44). Her own screen: 2, 44, 46 (the game draws its belt entity there).
# The host pins her 2.2 m ahead, her back to him (collision 6), then facing him (collision 3), each view with the rig off then
# on (the host's `gear 38 0/1`); then the optional parts (`gear 38 1 15`) for the look check, and back to the default; then
# her own screen (her front), rig off and on.
# covers: skeleton gear
role host
graphics low
timeout 300
launcherini local-test
start control 200
@guest waitlog 60 \[self\] showing 'fragile_suit' in place of Sam .*fit on
waitlog 60 \[sync\] partner body: real 'fragile_suit'
# the launcher's ini has no exo_rig line: on by default
waitlog 5 \[config\] exo_rig=1 exo_rig_extra=0
@guest waitlog 5 \[config\] exo_rig=1 exo_rig_extra=0
@both goto mknot-road
wait 6000
@guest pad none 2400 ly=32767
@guest pad none 900 ly=-32767
@guest waitlog 60 \[exo-self\] carrier [0-9A-Fa-fx]+: shown
@guest waitlog 30 \[exo-self\] rig \(section 41\): parts 2 44( 45)? 46( |$)
waitlog 60 \[exo\] carrier .* follows the partner body's pose
waitlog 60 \[exo\] carrier [0-9A-Fa-fx]+: shown
# the host's copy of her: the suit's belt and straps too
waitlog 30 \[exo\] rig \(section 41\): parts 2 25 26 27 28 44( 45)? 46( |$)
waitlog 30 \[exo\] carrier .* parts: .*shown now: [0-9 ]*\b25\b[0-9 ]*\b46\b.*rig on\)
# ---- her back (the host's own Sam to one side of the frame)
action collision 6 2.2
wait 2000

wait 1500
action gear 38 0
waitnew 10 \[exo\] carrier .* parts: .*rig off\)
wait 1200

waitnew 10 \[exo\] carrier .* parts: .*rig on\)
wait 1200
screenshot H1-back-on
# ---- her front: pinned at the same spot, facing him (the camera turned back for the pin, then as for her back)

wait 1500

wait 1500

wait 2000
action gear 38 0
waitnew 10 \[exo\] carrier .* parts: .*rig off\)
wait 1200

waitnew 10 \[exo\] carrier .* parts: .*rig on\)
wait 1200

action gear 38 1 0
waitnew 10 \[exo\] rig \(section 41\): parts 2 25 26 27 28 44( 45)? 46( |$)
# ---- her own screen (her front): rig off, on
@guest action gear 38 0
@guest waitnew 10 \[exo-self\] carrier .* parts: .*rig off\)

@guest screenshot G1-guest-self-off
