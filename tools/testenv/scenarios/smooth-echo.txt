# Smooth animation above 60 fps (docs/real-partner-body.md section 3.5), one game: the echo partner (a Sam

# caps 30, 60, 120 and none, first with the v0.7 behaviour (smooth_pose=0: bodies at the game's 59.94 Hz),
# then the 240 Hz boost alone, then everything (mask 7). The [smooth] line every second has the numbers:
