# The launcher's own Local test files. 1. The host drives the Kusanagi trike with the guest on its pillion: the hide is held
# (inventory +0x4C7C 0x03, the game's HideBackpack + HideSpareShoes) and a save asked of the game then (save_guard 1 4: its

# is written while the driver's hide is held. 2. The worst case on foot: the hide forced on (ride_test 36 1), a save
# written with it (an autosave into the run's scratch copy, its capture at once), the hide given back (ride_test 36 0),
# and that save loaded in the world (save_guard 3; the guest follows). Pass: the loaded Sam's inventory byte is 0x00 (the
# hide bits are never in the save), nothing held, the carrier's hide mask 0 (his cargo drawn, S2), no FAULT.
