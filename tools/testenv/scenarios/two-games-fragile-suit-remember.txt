# Round 5: the guest's own suit colour pick outlives the host's loads (docs/real-partner-body.md section 27.2), hidden, on the
# launcher's own Local test files. The guest's world is the host's save loaded into a sandbox that is never saved, so her
# Sam's suit colour byte comes back as the host's (the 100% save: gold, 16) at every load; the mod remembers her last pick
# (suit_colour_math GuestPick, in the run's own output folder) and Fragile keeps wearing it on both screens.
#   pwsh -File tools\testenv\Start-TestRun.ps1 -Scenario two-games-fragile-suit-remember -SeedSaves complete-100 -GuestSeed profile-only
# Pass: the guest picks palette 9 (`real_outfit 4 9`, the inventory byte the uniform menu sets; hidden tests only), both
# screens show 9; the host saves (its own autosave into the run's scratch copy) and loads it in the world, the guest follows
# (loads the host's save: her Sam is gold again) and logs that Fragile keeps the pick; after the load neither game puts gold
# back on the same Fragile body (`colour 16 on (#2..`), no FAULT.
role host
graphics low
timeout 360
# Round 6: the tint is the fallback option now (session.ini fragile_suit_colour, default 0): this scenario turns it on.
ini fragile_suit_colour=1
launcherini local-test
start control 200
@guest waitlog 60 \[self\] showing 'fragile_suit' in place of Sam .*fit on
waitlog 60 \[sync\] partner body: real 'fragile_suit'
@both goto mknot-road
wait 6000
waitlog 30 \[suit\] body .*: colour 16 on
@guest waitlog 30 \[suit\] body .*: colour 16 on
# the guest picks palette 9: remembered
@guest action real_outfit 4 9
@guest waitnew 15 \[suit\] the guest picked suit colour 9: remembered
@guest waitlog 15 \[suit\] body .*: colour 9 on
waitlog 15 \[suit\] body .*: colour 9 on
wait 1000
screenshot 01-host-sees-guest-9
@guest screenshot 01-guest-self-9
# ---- the host's game saves, then loads that save in the world; the guest loads it too (her Sam gold again)
until 30 1500 \[save\] #[0-9]+ pending \(forced save F[0-9]+\): auto save => action save_guard 1 4 1
waitlog 60 \[save\] finished ok: #[0-9]+ \(forced save F[0-9]+\)
wait 2000
action save_guard 3
waitnew 20 \[coop\] host: in-world Load \(type 0x4
@guest waitnew 120 \[coop\] guest: wrote the host's save #[0-9]+ .*
@guest waitnew 180 \[coop\] guest: in the world after .* save #[0-9]+
waitnew 120 \[coop\] host: the partner's RESULT for load #[0-9]+: ok
@guest waitlog 60 \[suit\] guest: world #[2-9] loaded with Sam's suit colour 16: Fragile keeps the guest's remembered pick 9
@both goto mknot-road
wait 8000
@guest action real_outfit 0
screenshot 02-host-sees-guest-after-load
@guest screenshot 02-guest-self-after-load
nolog \[suit\] body .*: colour 16 on \(#([2-9]|[1-9][0-9])
@guest nolog \[suit\] body .*: colour 16 on \(#([2-9]|[1-9][0-9])
nolog \[suit\] .*FAULTED
@guest nolog \[suit\] .*FAULTED
@both action log_status
@guest quit
wait 3000
quit
