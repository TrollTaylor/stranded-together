# Echo test of the partner body on the hidden desktop (Start-TestRun.ps1 -Scenario echo-holo):
# boot, Continue, walk ~6 s so the echo partner (your own movement replayed 2 s later, welcome
# hologram by default) trails a few metres behind, then turn the camera round to look back and
# keep walking towards the camera, with screenshots of the trailing hologram at a distance
# (it fades within 2 m of Sam, so it is only visible while Sam keeps moving).
#
# The sticks work in the world; what blocked them in the first runs was the save itself: right
# after this save loads (Incinerator West of Capital Knot City, Episode 1) Sam can walk for ~5 s,
# then a scripted BT scene ("We've got company") holds the player for ~13 s, and only then does
# the stick move him again (docs/hidden-testing.md "Pad input in gameplay"). So the scenario waits
# 17 s after the player appears and then probes with 1 s pushes until one moves Sam >= 1 m.
# Every pad result line says how far the player moved ("player moved 9.54 m") and what the
# game's own input manager saw ("slot0(... sticks=0.00,1.00,0.00,0.00)").
role echo
timeout 360
waitready 90
waitlog 120 \[overlay\] input attached
wait 25000
?screenshot 01-title
pad A 150
wait 4000
until 240 5000 \[sync\] local player entity 0+ -> 0*[1-9A-F] => pad A 120
waitplayer 60
# the scripted BT scene after loading this save holds the player for ~5..18 s after it appears
wait 17000
# control is back once a 1 s push moves Sam at least 1 m (ly=32760 marks the probe's log lines)
until 45 300 ly=32760 -> ok: .*player moved ([1-9]|[1-9][0-9]+)\.[0-9]+ m => pad none 1000 ly=32760
# The gate leaves Sam a few metres out on the apron, facing the BT field (walking out there gets
# him caught by BTs within seconds), the camera behind him. Turn the camera round first (right
# stick, ~1.3 s at full deflection is ~180 degrees) so "forward" leads back into the incinerator
# building, where no BTs come.
pad none 1300 rx=32767
screenshot 02-camera-round
# walk ~6 s into the building (left stick up); the echo partner follows 2 s behind, out of view
pad none 6000 ly=32767
waitlog 5 pad none 6000 ly=32767 -> ok: .*player moved ([5-9]|[1-9][0-9]+)\.[0-9]+ m
screenshot 03-walked
snapshot
# turn the camera to look back: the partner is still coming along Sam's path
pad none 1300 rx=32767
screenshot 04-looking-back
# keep walking the same way, now towards the camera: the hologram trails behind Sam, in view
pad none 1500 ly=-32767
screenshot 05-trailing-a
pad none 1500 ly=-32767
screenshot 06-trailing-b
pad none 1500 ly=-32767
screenshot 07-trailing-c
snapshot
# standing: the partner arrives within 2 s and fades
wait 3000
screenshot 08-stopped
action log_status
menu open
wait 700
screenshot 09-menu-status
menu close
wait 300
quit
