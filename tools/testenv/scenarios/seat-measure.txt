# Riding together (design M3.5 #1; docs/real-partner-body.md section 24.2), one game, hidden:
#   pwsh -File tools\testenv\Start-TestRun.ps1 -Scenario seat-measure -SeedSaves complete-100
# For each kind (truck, roadster, the Kusanagi trike): Sam drives it (X), `seat_measure` logs his root and pelvis in the
# vehicle's frame, the vehicle's joint 1 and the game's seat helpers (HLP_Driver / HLP_Passenger / HLP_C_Driver) through it,
# and keeps his seated pose; he gets off; `ride_test 1` carries him on that vehicle's passenger seat (the trike: the pillion)
# with the kept pose exactly as a passenger is carried (capsule off, the entity teleport every tick, the input blocked),
# the camera turned for a side view; `ride_test 2` puts him off beside it (the trike: `ride_test 9`, the emergency stop).
# Pass (in the logs): every kind measured, carried with a small seat error, put off with the capsule back on (counter back
# to its value), no fault. Check enlarged: the Mxx-carried screenshots (Sam sitting in the passenger seat / on the pillion, not outside, not standing on the seat).
