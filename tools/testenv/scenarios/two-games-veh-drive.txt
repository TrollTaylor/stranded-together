# Shared real vehicles, remote driving (design M2.10 #3; docs/real-partner-body.md sections 23.11 - 23.15), hidden:
#   pwsh -File tools\testenv\Start-TestRun.ps1 -Scenario two-games-veh-drive -SeedSaves complete-100 -GuestSeed profile-only
# host_save=1 on both games, vehicle_sync unset: the real shared vehicles (vehicle_sync=5) with remote driving. The host
# drives the truck with the game's own X and pad (straight, a turn, a hard stop, a reverse) while the guest stands 9 m
# beside it with the camera turned onto it (vehicle_sync 18). The watcher's game drives ITS OWN unit of that vehicle
# with the game's NPC driver controller, fed the driver's 48 input bytes (STD1) at the partner body's play time, and
# corrects it in bands (vehicle_drive 1 logs the placement error mean / max and the corrections per band; every second
# a "[vdrive] ... followed: placement error" line). Pass (in the logs): the follower attached and detached, errors under
# 0.5 m in normal driving, no teleport while driving straight, the partner body seated on the real vehicle (the
# screenshots), one unit per vehicle in each world (vehicle_dump 1 / 2 on both: the same ids, no new unit).
# Check enlarged: D01..07 pairs (wheels turning, the partner seated in the real vehicle, the vehicle where the driver's
# screen has it).
role host
graphics low
timeout 360
ini host_save=1
ini partner_body=real
ini partner_collision=0
waitready 90
@guest waitready 120
waitlog 120 \[overlay\] input attached
@guest waitlog 120 \[overlay\] input attached
waitlog 10 \[coop\] host-held save session ON \(host
@guest waitlog 10 \[coop\] host-held save session ON \(guest
waitlog 60 \[vshare\] shared real vehicles ON \(host
@guest waitlog 60 \[vshare\] shared real vehicles ON \(guest
waitlog 10 \[vdrive\] remote driving ON \(host
@guest waitlog 10 \[vdrive\] remote driving ON \(guest
# the title menus (~20 s after the first frame); the host Continues (CONTINUE is selected on its title)
wait 22000
waitlog 30 \[link\] CONNECTED
pad A 150
waitlog 30 \[coop\] host: title Continue
@guest waitlog 120 \[coop\] guest: wrote the host's save #1 as manualsave5
until 240 5000 \[sync\] local player entity 0+ -> 0*[1-9A-F] => pad A 120
waitplayer 60
@guest waitlog 180 \[coop\] guest: in the world after
@guest waitplayer 60
waitlog 60 \[vshare\] host: world ready
@guest waitlog 60 \[vshare\] guest: world ready
@guest waitlog 60 \[vshare\] guest: the host's snapshot #[0-9]+ is complete
@guest waitlog 30 \[vshare\] guest: binding:
wait 12000
# ---- host drives the truck, guest watches from beside it (the camera NEAR the vehicle)
action vehicle_sync 8 1.8 2
wait 1500
@guest action vehicle_sync 8 9 2
wait 2000
@guest action vehicle_sync 18 2
@guest until 25 150 camera aimed #1 => pad none 200 rx=32767
action vehicle_dump 1
@guest action vehicle_dump 1

@guest waitlog 20 \[vdrive\] follower of truck 0x[0-9A-F]+ attached
