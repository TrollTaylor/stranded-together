# Fragile's porter suit in Sam's suit colours between two games on the launcher's own Local test files (docs/real-partner-
# body.md section 27), hidden. The guest plays Fragile in her porter suit (the launcher's default); her suit wears the
# guest's own Sam's suit colour: on the guest's screen (her self view) and on the host's (his partner Fragile), from the
# STJ1 outfit the guest sends. host_save: the guest's Sam starts as a copy of the host's (the 100% save: gold, 16), so she
# starts gold on both screens. Then the guest changes its own Sam's suit colour (`real_outfit 4 <c>`, the inventory byte
# the uniform menu sets; hidden tests only): silver, palette 9, 1, 13, her own look (0), gold again; both screens follow.
# The host's own Sam stays as he was (gold) throughout: his outfit lines, and the guest's partner Sam.
#   pwsh -File tools\testenv\Start-TestRun.ps1 -Scenario two-games-fragile-suit -SeedSaves complete-100 -GuestSeed profile-only

launcherini local-test
