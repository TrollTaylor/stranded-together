# Two games, the guest as the launcher sets it up by default (docs/real-partner-body.md sections 16-17): the host
# plays Sam; the guest Fragile in her porter suit (my_character=fragile_suit, self_character=1: what the launcher
# writes for every guest unless Debug > "Allow other characters" is on), the self view fit and head lift on,
# the cap on. Both cameras look at the other player's front: gear fit (cap, shoulder shields, arm and hip cargo),
# boots, then weapons drawn, aimed, fired and put away on both screens; the guest's back from the host; the
# guest's own front; the self view off and on again; last the leak check (the guest runs with the fit on, then
# off). For more detail run it with a 1080p settings.cfg:
#   pwsh -File tools\testenv\Start-TestRun.ps1 -Scenario two-games-final -SeedSaves complete-100 -SettingsCfg <1080p cfg>
role host
graphics low
timeout 360
ini partner_body=real
@host ini my_character=sam
@guest ini my_character=fragile_suit
@guest ini self_character=1
waitready 90
@guest waitready 120
waitlog 120 \[overlay\] input attached
@guest waitlog 120 \[overlay\] input attached
wait 25000
pad A 150
@guest pad A 150
wait 4000
until 240 5000 \[sync\] local player entity 0+ -> 0*[1-9A-F] => pad A 120
@guest until 240 5000 \[sync\] local player entity 0+ -> 0*[1-9A-F] => pad A 120
waitplayer 60
@guest waitplayer 60
waitlog 60 \[link\] CONNECTED
@guest waitlog 60 \[link\] CONNECTED
until 120 500 ly=32760 -> ok: .*player moved ([1-9]|[1-9][0-9]+)\.[0-9]+ m => pad none 1000 ly=32760
@guest until 150 3000 \[sync\] partner body: real 'sam' => pad A 120
waitlog 60 \[sync\] partner body: real 'fragile_suit'
@guest waitlog 30 \[self\] showing 'fragile_suit' in place of Sam .*fit on
# the cap on the guest (the game puts it on itself; scratch saves only)
@guest action gear 14 15 1
waitlog 40 \[gear\] [0-9]+ items, [1-9][0-9]* props live
@guest pad none 2600 lx=14000 ly=32767
@guest pad none 1300 rx=32767
@guest pad none 350 ly=32767

@guest pad none 0
wait 1500
@guest pad dpad_right 600 keep
