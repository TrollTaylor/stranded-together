# Fragile's accessories while she moves (docs/real-partner-body.md section 26), one game at 30 fps (the cap two games run
# at): our self view (Fragile in her porter suit in place of Sam: the game draws Sam's own cap, glasses, backpack, cargo
# and cases on his hidden fitted skeleton) and the echo partner (the same Fragile, 0.3 s behind and 1.2 m aside, wearing
# our gear as props: what the other screen shows) in every picture. The self body follows Sam three ways, each while
# running across the camera's view: follow 1 (v0.18: Sam's world copied in his own update, after the body's), 2 (copied
# in the body's own update: the previous frame's) and 4 (v0.19: Sam's world predicted for this frame in the body's
# update, then the copy). Then walking, crouching and turning, follow 1 against 4. The `[self] follow 4:` line says how
# many body updates got the prediction and how long the step was.
#   pwsh -File tools\testenv\Start-TestRun.ps1 -Scenario fragile-attach -SeedSaves complete-100 [-SettingsCfg <1440p cfg>]
# covers: gear self-view partner-body

timeout 360
ini partner_body=real
ini partner_character=fragile_suit
