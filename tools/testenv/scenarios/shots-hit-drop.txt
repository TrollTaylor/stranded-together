# BulletDamageA / B before any receiver of this world sees them (our own Sam excepted: the Sam filter keeps its policy).
# Solo on the 100% save, its assault rifle. The aimed test (shots 5 <target> <n>) replays n shots of our own last captured
# shot (kind 4, a partner replay for every filter) from 6 m in front of a target, straight at it, and reads its value before
# and 2.5 s after; a negative n is the control (their damage let through at the bullet). Targets: a dev truck (its life), a
# dev postbox (its durability), and at the MULE camp the enemy probe's MULE (counters only: at the bullet "npc"; the control's
# hits never reach the Humanoid backstop, run 20261124-093844). The class counters are the evidence: the non-lethal rifle
# wears neither the truck nor the postbox even in the control (run 20261124-092442), so "unchanged" alone proves nothing. The
# BT reaction's drop is off by default (shots_bt_drop=0).
# No cargo step (dropped in round 16): shots 6 has no piece a replay can hit. Near the camp it picks the nearest DSBaggage2
# with an entity, 46-47 m off (runs 20261124-093844, -115147), and the 4 aimed replays hit no receiver either time (cargo +0,
# every class +0): its stored place (+0x38) is likely not where the realized piece is (the camp's MULEs carry their own cargo,
# a "Ceramics (80)" tag in the still). At mknot-road there is no such piece and the carried piece it puts down keeps no entity
# (run 20261124-092442). The step was optional, and a failed optional step fails the run, so the probe never passed. Every
# bullet hit still goes through BulletDamageA / B, so a piece's hit would be dropped like the truck's; it stays unproven
# (docs/enemy-sync.md 7.5) until a target a replay really hits exists (a spawned piece at a known place).
