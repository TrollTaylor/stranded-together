# Walking on the hidden desktop (Start-TestRun.ps1 -Scenario walk): boot, Continue, then the
# virtual pad's left stick walks Sam and the right stick turns the camera.
# Checked by the scenario: the 6 s walk must move the player >= 5 m (each pad result line says
# "player moved X m" and what the game's own input manager saw, "sticks=LX,LY,RX,RY"). The
# camera turns are visible in the screenshots (02 -> 03 -> 04 face different ways).
#
# This save (Incinerator West of Capital Knot City, Episode 1) holds the player in a scripted BT
# scene from ~5 s to ~18 s after it appears ("We've got company"): sticks pushed then do nothing,
# which is what the first hidden runs ran into (docs/hidden-testing.md "Pad input in gameplay").
# So: wait 17 s, then 1 s pushes until one moves Sam >= 1 m. Out on the apron BTs catch Sam
# within seconds, so the walk goes back into the incinerator building (camera turned round first).
# covers: boot
role solo
timeout 300
waitready 90
waitlog 120 \[overlay\] input attached
wait 25000
?screenshot 01-title
pad A 150
wait 4000
until 240 5000 \[sync\] local player entity 0+ -> 0*[1-9A-F] => pad A 120
waitplayer 60
wait 17000
until 45 300 ly=32760 -> ok: .*player moved ([1-9]|[1-9][0-9]+)\.[0-9]+ m => pad none 1000 ly=32760
snapshot
screenshot 02-control
# camera round (~180 degrees: right stick ~1.3 s at full deflection)
pad none 1300 rx=32767
screenshot 03-camera-round
# walk 6 s (left stick up) into the building
pad none 6000 ly=32767
waitlog 5 pad none 6000 ly=32767 -> ok: .*player moved ([5-9]|[1-9][0-9]+)\.[0-9]+ m
snapshot
screenshot 04-walked
# a quarter turn of the camera to the left while standing
pad none 650 rx=-32767
screenshot 05-turned-left
snapshot
quit
