# drives the truck and the guest's Fragile rides as the passenger (the game's own Use Vehicle). Both games run the boots
# meter (ride_test 34 1: "[ride] boots meter: life ..., on-foot flag, move level, speed, wear events by type" every second)
# and take marks (ride_test 34 2: "[ride] boots mark #n: life ... (unchanged|dropped ... since mark #n-1)").
# Controls: the guest stands 15 s (unchanged), walks 14 s (dropped). Lap A: the boots guard OFF (ride_test 35 0, v0.21):
# the host's autopilot drives play test 3's road at 7 m/s (~65 s); the ride's summary "[ride] boots over the ride on the
# truck: life a -> b, LOST|unchanged; ... wear events j/t/l/s/p" names what the game charged. Lap B: fresh boots again
# (dev_boots 1 100), the guard ON (default): the same road, the summary must say unchanged (and 1.5 s after getting off).
# Then the guest walks 14 s again: dropped (the guard ended). The host drove both laps: its own boots unchanged (a driver
# wears none, vanilla).
# Pass: every step ok (marks #2 unchanged / #3 dropped / #5 dropped on the guest, the host's mark #3 unchanged, lap B
# unchanged with skipped ticks), no FAULT, summary.json result pass.
