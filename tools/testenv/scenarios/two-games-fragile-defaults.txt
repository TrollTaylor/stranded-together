# Fragile's ready-made suit colours between two games (round 8; docs/textures.md "Ready-made colours", STT1 RECIPE),
# hidden, on the launcher's own Local test files. The run's own freshly extracted textures folder (both games'
# session.ini name it), nothing painted. Noon, clear, on the knot road; the host's camera on the guest's Fragile pinned
# ahead of it (collision 3). The guest (Fragile) wears the ready-made colour of her Sam's suit colour and announces its
# blobs as a RECIPE; the host makes the same blobs itself from its own copy of her Original pictures (checked by
# SHA-256): no transfer. host_save: the guest's Sam starts gold (16: S0 and S1). Then mustard (9: S0). Then a quick run of
# colour changes on a slow host (tex 12 3: each make 2 s slower): the older looks' makes are dropped ("no longer made
# here") and the last colour (14) shows without waiting for them. Then the fallback: the host's make throws (tex 12 4, as
# an out-of-memory would): its request still reports, and the guest's sky blue (13) comes over the link (about 60 s).
# Gold again: the host's partner cache has both blobs.
#   pwsh -File tools\testenv\Run-Batch.ps1 -Scenarios two-games-fragile-defaults -SeedSaves complete-100 -GuestSeed profile-only
role host
graphics low
timeout 360
launcherini local-test
textures extract
start control 200
@guest waitlog 60 \[self\] showing 'fragile_suit' in place of Sam .*fit on
waitlog 60 \[sync\] partner body: real 'fragile_suit'
@both goto mknot-road
wait 6000
@both ?action set_time_of_day 12
@both ?action dev_weather 0
@guest pad none 2400 ly=32767
@guest pad none 900 ly=-32767
@guest waitlog 60 \[tex\] look .* for colour 16 .*: ready-made \(S0 S1\)
@guest waitlog 30 \[tex\] look #[0-9]+ sent: colour 16, 2 blob\(s\) \(2 ready-made: recipe sent\)
waitlog 30 \[tex\] partner ready-made S0 .* made here in
waitlog 30 \[tex\] partner ready-made S1 .* made here in
waitlog 30 \[tex\] partner look .*: complete for colour 16 .*made here 2
waitlog 30 \[tex\] body partner .*: look .* bound .* kept [1-9]
action collision 3 1.6
pad none 220 rx=32767
wait 1200
action collision 3 1.3
pad none 150 rx=-32767
wait 1800
@both action tex 0
screenshot 00-host-sees-guest-gold
@guest screenshot 00-guest-self-gold
# mustard: S0 only, made here
@guest action real_outfit 4 9
@guest waitnew 30 \[tex\] look #[0-9]+ sent: colour 9, 1 blob\(s\) \(1 ready-made: recipe sent\)
waitnew 60 \[tex\] partner ready-made S0 .* made here in
waitnew 30 \[tex\] partner look .*: complete for colour 9 .*made here 1
waitnew 30 \[tex\] body partner .*: look .* bound .* kept [1-9]
wait 1500
screenshot 01-host-sees-guest-mustard
@guest screenshot 01-guest-self-mustard
# a quick run of colour changes on a slow host: the newest look waits only for its own blob
action tex 12 3
@guest action real_outfit 4 1
wait 700
@guest action real_outfit 4 2
wait 700
@guest action real_outfit 4 3
wait 700
@guest action real_outfit 4 4
wait 700
@guest action real_outfit 4 14
waitnew 30 \[tex\] partner look #[0-9]+: [0-9]+ ready-made blob\(s\) of an older look no longer made here
waitnew 40 \[tex\] partner look .*: complete for colour 14 .*made here 1
action tex 12 0
waitnew 30 \[tex\] body partner .*: look .* bound .* kept [1-9]
wait 1500
screenshot 02-host-sees-guest-pink
@guest screenshot 02-guest-self-pink
# the fallback: this game's make throws (as out of memory): the request still gets its result and the guest sends the blob
action tex 12 4
@guest action real_outfit 4 13
waitnew 60 \[tex\] partner ready-made S0 .* not made here \(out of memory while making it\): asking the guest for it
waitnew 180 \[tex\] partner look .*: received S0 .* valid
waitnew 30 \[tex\] partner look .*: complete for colour 13
waitnew 30 \[tex\] body partner .*: look .* bound .* kept [1-9]
wait 1500
screenshot 03-host-sees-guest-sky-blue
@guest screenshot 03-guest-self-sky-blue
# gold again: both blobs are in the host's partner cache
@guest action real_outfit 4 16
waitnew 60 \[tex\] partner look .*: complete for colour 16 .*cache hit 2
waitnew 30 \[tex\] body partner .*: look .* bound .* kept [1-9]
wait 1500
screenshot 04-host-sees-guest-gold-again
action collision 3 0
@both action log_status
@both action tex 0
nolog \[tex\] .*FAULTED
@guest nolog \[tex\] .*FAULTED
nolog \[tex\] ready-made colours off
@guest nolog \[tex\] ready-made colours off
nolog \[tex\] refused
@guest nolog \[tex\] host refused
nolog \[tex\] .*NOT valid
@guest quit
wait 3000
quit
