# Shared real vehicles, remote driving (design M2.10 #3; docs/real-partner-body.md sections 23.11 - 23.15), hidden:
#   pwsh -File tools\testenv\Start-TestRun.ps1 -Scenario two-games-veh-drive-guest -SeedSaves complete-100 -GuestSeed profile-only
# host_save=1 on both games, vehicle_sync unset: the real shared vehicles (vehicle_sync=5) with remote driving. The guest
# drives the truck with the game's own X and pad (straight, a turn, a hard stop, a reverse) while the host stands 9 m
# beside it with the camera turned onto it (vehicle_sync 18). The watcher's game drives ITS OWN unit of that vehicle
# with the game's NPC driver controller, fed the driver's 48 input bytes (STD1) at the partner body's play time, and
# corrects it in bands (vehicle_drive 1 logs the placement error mean / max and the corrections per band; every second
# a "[vdrive] ... followed: placement error" line). Pass (in the logs): the follower attached and detached, errors under
# 0.5 m in normal driving, no teleport while driving straight, the partner body seated on the real vehicle (the
# screenshots), one unit per vehicle in each world (vehicle_dump 1 / 2 on both: the same ids, no new unit).
# Check enlarged: G01..07 pairs (wheels turning, the partner seated in the real vehicle, the vehicle where the driver's
# screen has it).
# covers: vehicle-share

ini partner_body=real
ini partner_collision=0

@guest waitlog 120 \[overlay\] input attached

# the title menus (~20 s after the first frame); the host Continues (CONTINUE is selected on its title)

@guest waitplayer 60

@guest waitlog 60 \[vshare\] guest: world ready
