# Partner sounds, Phase 3, two games (docs/audio-research.md section 16): the host plays Sam, the guest Fragile in her
# porter suit with the self view (what the launcher writes by default), self_voice=auto and voice_sync=1 on both,
# sound_sync=1 and the audio probe on both (the probe owns the Wwise hooks and forwards to the partner sounds).
#   pwsh -File tools\testenv\Start-TestRun.ps1 -Scenario two-games-sound -SeedSaves complete-100
# Every mirrored post is logged on both sides (`sound_sync 1 1`).
# 1. The host walks, runs, jumps and fires the rifle: his footsteps, foley and gun go out as STA1; the guest posts them
#    on her Wwise objects at the host body's joints at the pose play time (playing id, end-of-event callbacks).
# 2. The guest does the same: the other direction.
# 3. The guest opens the cuff map while the host runs: the guest posts nothing while it is open (her game's gate) and
#    stops what was playing (A dismisses the map's notice, B closes the map); the host then opens his while the guest runs.
# covers: sound
role host
graphics low
timeout 360
ini partner_body=real
ini voice_sync=1
ini sound_sync=1
ini audio_probe=1
ini self_voice=auto
@host ini my_character=sam
@guest ini my_character=fragile_suit
@guest ini self_character=1
waitready 90
@guest waitready 120
waitlog 120 \[overlay\] input attached
@guest waitlog 120 \[overlay\] input attached
wait 25000
pad A 150
@guest pad A 150
wait 4000
until 240 5000 \[sync\] local player entity 0+ -> 0*[1-9A-F] => pad A 120
@guest until 240 5000 \[sync\] local player entity 0+ -> 0*[1-9A-F] => pad A 120
waitplayer 60
@guest waitplayer 60
waitlog 60 \[link\] CONNECTED
@guest waitlog 60 \[link\] CONNECTED

@guest until 150 3000 \[sync\] partner body: real 'sam' => pad A 120
waitlog 60 \[sync\] partner body: real 'fragile_suit'
@guest waitlog 30 \[self\] showing 'fragile_suit' in place of Sam
wait 2000
action sound_sync 1 1
@guest action sound_sync 1 1
screenshot 01-host-sees-guest
@guest screenshot 01-guest-sees-host
